Dearest Princess Akhsa,

"Once I thought about a little sis, but He gave me a sis as you." 👑💖

Today is Princess Day, and there is honestly no one in the world more deserving of being celebrated than you! Having you in my life as my cherished soul sister and darling little sis is one of the greatest blessings I could ever ask for.

You don’t need glass slippers or a castle on a hill to be true royalty. The royal crown you wear every single day is woven from your endless kindness, your gentle heart, your radiant smile, and that sparkling laughter that brightens even the cloudiest days.

We may live in different houses, but you hold a permanent throne in my heart. Watching you grow, cheering for your dreams, protecting your peace, listening to your late-night rants, and sharing our crazy inside jokes are some of my favorite joys in life.

Never let anyone dull your sparkle or make you doubt your magic. Always remember who you are: a girl with a heart of gold, a spirit full of fire, and a big sister who will always be standing right beside you to protect your crown.

On this special Princess Day, I want to promise you that no matter how busy life gets, you will always have my unconditional love, my guidance, and your biggest cheerleader in me.

Happy Princess Day to my favorite soul sister and forever princess! Keep shining, keep ruling, and keep wearing your crown proudly. 👑✨

With all my royal love & endless hugs,
